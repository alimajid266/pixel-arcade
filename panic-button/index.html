YOUR JOB

Read the CRT order and provide the matching button input before the timer empties.

WAIT / DO NOTHING: touch nothing. This succeeds when the timer ends.

AUTH INVALID: the order is forged. Touch nothing.

FIRST TWO ORDERS ARE SAFE TRAINING · HEADPHONES RECOMMENDED
FORM PB-01

OPERATOR FIELD MANUAL

OBJECTIVE: Keep INTEGRITY above 0%. Skipping a binding active order costs 8–12 integrity depending on complexity. WAIT, DO NOTHING, and forged orders require no input.

CHANNEL TRUST: COMPUTER, SUPERVISOR, and EMERGENCY orders are always valid. UNKNOWN and YOU are always forged. When authorization is hidden, use this source rule or spend VERIFY.

VERIFY: Once per order, the Panic Button reveals whether the current order is real and grants 1.2 seconds. It does not damage integrity.

PROTOCOL 03 — BREACHRounds 6–9 · Sequences, delays, and visible forged orders.
PROTOCOL 04 — BLACKOUTRounds 10–14 · Authorization is hidden. Judge the channel or Verify.
PROTOCOL 05 — COLLAPSERound 15+ · Fastest timer, three-button sequences, highest-complexity orders and maximum interference.
